Subtraction is the process of taking away one number from another. It tells you how much is left, or the difference between the two numbers
The basic form is a-b=c
a= the number you start with
b= the number your taking away
c= the number, called the difference

Ex: Lets say you have ten apples and you take away 4 apples.
10-4=6
you moved backwards 4 times from 10
